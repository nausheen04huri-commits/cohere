09 / Failures, Limitations & What Could Change the Conclusion

Record caveats instead of treating a single result as universal.

Run the experiment to populate the validation report. Results are limited to the selected payoff model, strategy rules, round count, and noise settings.
  • Potential failures to watch: invalid payoff ordering, zero rounds, missing history rows, or a result that changes substantially across noise conditions.
  • Limitations: only two agents interact; strategies are simplified; the payoff values are chosen by the model; 100 seeded trials are useful but do not prove universal behaviour.
  • What could change the conclusion: more seeds, different payoff matrices, more rounds, additional strategy types, or independent validation against a hand-calculated example.
